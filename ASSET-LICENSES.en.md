| `Resources/RawData`, `Resources/Tables`, `Resources/Data` | Extracted/decoded original maps, tables, and values, plus extension data. Original/additional-row comparisons: `Resources/level10-extension.json` |
| `Resources/Generated` | Glyph atlas reproducing the original text display. Font files are not included |
| `Art`, `Apple/iOS/Assets.xcassets`, platform icons, and extended images | Concepts, icons, and additional parts based on original artwork. Final turret art: [production manifest](Art/Level10/20261107/runtime-art.json) |
| Game captures in `docs/evidence/images`, `docs/editorial/assets`, and reports | Original, port, and restored-game screens. Follow the platform, build, and date in each validation record |
